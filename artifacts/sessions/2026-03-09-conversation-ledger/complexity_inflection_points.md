# Complexity Inflection Points

## Decision Point

Expanding the system boundary to include both the software and the team operating it.

## Before

Architecture treated primarily as:
- software structure
- platform diagrams
- forward-looking strategies

## After

Architecture includes:
- team operational capacity
- decision artifacts
- reasoning lineage from experiments

## Why Complexity Increased or Decreased

Conceptual complexity increased, but long-term operational complexity may decrease due to preserved learning and traceable decisions.

## Trigger Signals

- repeated rediscovery of known problems
- architecture plans failing due to team capacity
- loss of context for past decisions
