# Research Bridge Artifact

## System

liveview

## Session Focus

Exploring artifact-driven development and deterministic artifact lineage as a mechanism for preserving knowledge from experimental implementations.

## Evidence

Observations from the session:

- AI-assisted development enables rapid regeneration of implementations.
- Integration friction often reveals architectural misalignment.
- Teams frequently discard experimental code without preserving reasoning artifacts.

## Observed Pattern

When code is disposable but artifacts are preserved, experimentation becomes a durable knowledge process.

## Candidate Observation

Artifact lineage can convert throwaway experimentation into structured system learning.

## Open Questions

- How granular should artifact capture be?
- Can team decision-state be captured without excessive overhead?
- What signals determine when an experiment graduates into the core system?
